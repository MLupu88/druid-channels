Continue the existing DRUID Partner Radar Lite application.

DO NOT rebuild it.
DO NOT add a large new architecture.
DO NOT turn this into a CRM, project-management tool, partner portal or marketing automation platform.

The goal is simple:

Help the Channels team:
1. find good new partners
2. know what changed around existing partners
3. spot useful RFPs, events, customer wins and AI activity
4. keep the Head of Channels’ existing reporting view trustworthy

PAMs already manage relationships through meetings, Teams, WhatsApp and email.
HubSpot remains the commercial system of record.
This app should reduce research work and improve visibility.

==================================================
3. STOP USING FAKE KPI CALCULATIONS
==================================================

Review the current KPI logic.

Do not calculate one KPI from something unrelated.

Examples of what must NOT happen:

- MQLs calculated from partner prospects
- Opportunities Created calculated from accepted partner prospects
- Events calculated from campaign count
- Renewals calculated from meetings
- Partner Referrals inferred from replies unless that is actually how the metric is defined

For the mockup, a metric may be:

- Mock data
- Manual data
- Connected data
- Not connected

It is better to show "--" than a misleading number.

The UI may show a small "Demo data" label while we are still in mock mode.

==================================================
17. SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

HEAD OF CHANNELS

Can open Overview and immediately see the exact reporting tables requested.

Can click a metric and understand what changed.

Does not need to learn another dashboard.

PAM

Can review a suggested new partner in under one minute.

Can open an existing partner and quickly understand:

- what changed
- relevant customer activity
- possible RFPs
- relevant events
- AI/business updates
- what help might be needed from HQ

PAM does not feel like they are maintaining another CRM.

PRODUCT

Feels simple.
Feels useful.
Reduces research time.
Surfaces information the team might otherwise miss.
Does not pretend to know things it cannot know.

The operating principle is:

Research finds something useful.
The PAM decides whether it matters.
HubSpot holds the commercial truth.
The Head of Channels sees what changed.
